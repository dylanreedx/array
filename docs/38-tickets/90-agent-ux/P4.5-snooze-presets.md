# P4.5 — Snooze presets, DST-safe
Phase: 4 · Depends on: P4.1 · Tag: autonomous · Execution-mode: medium

## Goal
"Not now, but come back" — the piece no competitor has, and the difference between deferring and
forgetting.

## Files
- `Sources/ContinuumRevivedAgentUI/SnoozePresets.swift` (new, pure)
- `Sources/ContinuumRevivedAgentUIChecks/SnoozePresetChecks.swift` (new)

## Approach
Four presets, deliberately **short** — agent rhythms are hours (a CI run, a review, the next work
session), not days:
- **In 1 hour**
- **This evening** — 18:00 local, *suppressed if less than 1h away*
- **Tomorrow** — 09:00 local
- **Next week** — next Monday 09:00 local

Compute with `Calendar`/`DateComponents` in the **local** timezone, never by adding seconds — that is
what breaks across a DST boundary. Pure function taking `now` + `Calendar` so it is testable.

## Done when
Presets resolve correctly, including across a DST transition, and the evening option hides when near.

## Verify
Checks with a fixed `Calendar` and injected `now`: each preset's wall-clock target; "this evening"
suppressed at 17:30 and offered at 09:00; **a DST spring-forward and fall-back date where naive
+86400 would land at the wrong wall-clock hour** (that comparison IS the witness); next-week from a
Monday and from a Sunday both land on the following Monday.

## Watch out
- Do not use `now.addingTimeInterval(3600 * 24)` for "tomorrow". That is the DST bug.
- Presets are UI copy + a date; do not wire the shelf or the wake logic here (P4.6/P4.7).
