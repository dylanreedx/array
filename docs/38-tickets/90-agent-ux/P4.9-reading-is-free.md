# P4.9 — Reading is free; only acting un-settles
Phase: 4 · Depends on: P4.4, P3.9 · Tag: autonomous · Execution-mode: low

## Goal
Opening settled work to look at it must not resurrect it. Otherwise reviewing your own history
un-files everything you filed.

## Files
- `Sources/ContinuumRevived/App/AgentInboxView.swift`
- `Sources/ContinuumRevived/App/AgentSupervisor.swift`

## Approach
Reveal/open (P3.9) is plain navigation: attach a view if needed, focus it, clear `unread` — and leave
`settledOverride` untouched. Only **acting** un-settles: sending a prompt, resolving an approval,
starting a session, or an explicit un-settle.

## Done when
Opening a settled agent leaves it settled; sending it a prompt un-settles it.

## Verify
Check: open a settled agent → still `.settled` and it stays in the settled tail (**witness**: opening
promoting it to active must FAIL); then send a prompt → `.neutral`/active.

## Watch out
- Clearing `unread` on open is correct and unrelated to lifecycle — do not couple them.
