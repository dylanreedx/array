# P4.12 — Crossfade lifecycle transitions in place
Phase: 4 · Depends on: P3.7, P4.7 · Tag: autonomous · Execution-mode: low

## Goal
A settle should read as the row *changing*, not as a card flying down the list. Rows here are
translucent, so a card sliding across other rows reads as text painted over text.

## Files
- `Sources/ContinuumRevived/App/AgentInboxView.swift`

## Approach
Key each row view by `id:variant` (P3.7). On a lifecycle change the card fades out **in place** while
the slim row fades in at its settled position — no FLIP animation through the intervening rows. Keep
it short (~150–200ms) and cancel cleanly if another change lands mid-animation.

## Done when
Settling crossfades rather than sliding, and rapid successive settles do not leave ghosts.

## Verify
Advisory via `--ui-tour-check` renders (animation is not gate-able), plus a deterministic check that
the view registry has exactly one view per `id:variant` after a transition (no orphaned card view).

## Watch out
- Do not animate during a full reload or on first render.
- Respect Reduce Motion: fall back to an instant swap.
