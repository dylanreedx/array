# P6.0 — Prose is not a card

Phase: 6 · Depends: P1.11

**PULLED FORWARD OUT OF PHASE ORDER at the owner's explicit instruction (2026-07-26).** This is a
correction of a design defect the owner reported on sight, not a new feature. It is ahead of the
rest of Phase 4 for that reason. Read `## Why this is out of order` before touching the queue.

## Goal

Today every transcript entry — an assistant sentence, a user sentence, a tool call, a plan, a diff,
an error — is rendered by one view (`TranscriptCardView`) as a **full-width rounded rectangle with a
1pt border, a bold title line, and a monospace status word**. The owner's words, unedited:

> "we have these odd response boxes? that are spanning the entire width of the tile each response"

They are right, and the defect is mine. A conversation rendered as a uniform stack of titled
bordered records reads as a **log viewer**, not a chat: every turn is boxed and captioned at equal
visual weight, so prose — the thing the user actually reads — competes with its own metadata, and
the eye has no path through the transcript.

After this ticket:

- **`.message` and `.userMessage` carry NO border, NO fill, and NO title row.** An assistant turn is
  bare prose on the tile body. A user turn is distinguished from it *without* chrome — see
  `## Approach` for the one permitted device and why it is not a bubble.
- **`.toolCall`, `.plan`, `.diff`, `.error` keep the card**, title and status word included. These
  are structured records; a bordered container is the correct reading of them, and the contrast
  between "prose flows, records are boxed" is what gives the transcript its shape.

The success criterion is comparative, and it is the owner's eye, not a number: at a glance the tile
must read as a conversation with a few boxed artifacts in it, not as a table of rows.

## Why this is out of order

The owner chose "pull the tile fixes forward now" when told the alternative was ~25 tickets of
Phase 4 and Phase 5 first. This packet needs nothing from Phase 4 (lifecycle), nothing from Phase 5
(RPC), and nothing from the rest of Phase 6 (compose surface): it is pure presentation over
`ManagedTranscriptCard`, which has shipped since ticket 88. Its only real dependency is the design
tokens (`P1.11`), long done. **Do not** pull anything else forward as a consequence of this.

## Approach

**Reuse, do not invent.** `ManagedTranscriptCard` and `ManagedTranscriptCardKind` in
`Sources/ContinuumRevivedCore/ManagedAgentTranscriptModel.swift` are unchanged by this ticket — the
model already distinguishes the kinds; only the view conflates them. Do not add a field to the model
to carry presentation. The kind IS the presentation key.

1. **Split the view, keep the seam.** Add `TranscriptProseView` beside `TranscriptCardView` in the
   same file, and have the tile pick by kind. Both must expose the SAME two entry points the tile
   and the probes already rely on — an `init(card:)` and an `apply(_ card:)` — and both must set
   `identifier` to `NSUserInterfaceItemIdentifier("managedAgent.card.\(card.id)")`. **That
   identifier string is load-bearing:** `UIProbeAppearance`, `UIProbePixels` and their recorded
   red-green witnesses all name views as `managedAgent.card.assistant-1`. Changing the spelling
   would silently un-probe the transcript, which is the P0-era failure this program exists to
   prevent. A shared protocol for the two views is fine if it earns its place; two classes with the
   same two methods is also fine. Do not build a factory abstraction for two cases.

2. **What `TranscriptProseView` is.** `wantsLayer` with **no** `cornerRadius`, **no** `borderWidth`,
   and **no** `backgroundColor` for `.message`. One `NSTextField(wrappingLabelWithString:)` on
   `.token(.body)` in `TextToken.textPrimary`, `isSelectable = true`, inset by `Inset.card`
   horizontally so prose does not collide with the tile edge. No title label. No status label.

3. **How a user turn differs from an assistant turn — the one permitted device.** `.userMessage`
   gets a **fill only**: `SurfaceToken.cardUserMessage`, no border, no title, corner radius
   `Radius.card`. Not a right-aligned bubble, and not a narrower column. Reasons, both measured
   constraints rather than taste:

   **Superseded by A1 (`.plans/45`, 2026-08-24, kept for history — do not edit the fill decision
   below).** Dylan's later call: the fill is gone entirely. `UserPromptView` now paints no
   background at all — `nil`, no corner radius — and carries only a ~2 pt `AgentLineRole.authorship`
   rule down its left edge (`LineWidth.rule`, a new stroke-slot token; not `Radius.card`, not
   `SurfaceToken.cardUserMessage`). The bubble-width and right-alignment reasoning below still holds
   and is why the rule route was taken instead of any bubble shape. See
   `docs/38-tickets/91-agent-tile-ux/_DESIGN.md` §11 and `docs/38-tickets/90-agent-ux/_LEDGER.md`.
   - The tile is resizable and often narrow (the sidebar is 320pt; tiles get smaller). A bubble that
     is 70% of width has no minimum at which it still reads as a bubble, and at ~200pt it is
     indistinguishable from the full-width box this ticket is removing.
   - Right-alignment would put the user's text against the tile's trailing edge where the status
     dot and corner overlay live (`CornerOverlayView`), which is a collision the geometry gate will
     fail and which no inset fixes at small widths.

   A fill with no border and no caption is enough to say "you said this" and costs no layout.

4. **Keep the status word where it means something.** `.toolCall`/`.plan`/`.diff`/`.error` keep
   `statusText(for:)` — "running"/"done"/"failed"/"declined" is real information about a record. An
   assistant sentence has no such status, and `statusText` already returns `""` when
   `card.status == nil`, so prose loses nothing by having no status label at all.

5. **`bodyFallback` follows the split.** The two prose kinds already return `""` from
   `bodyFallback(for:)`, so an empty prose card renders as an empty view — correct, and it must not
   render an empty bordered box, which is what it does today.

6. **Register the new view for theming.** `TranscriptProseView` paints token colours, so add
   `"TranscriptProseView"` to `UIProbeAppearance.tokenAdoptedOwners`. The comment above that set
   states the rule: *"an owner that is neither adopted nor listed is a failure"*. A new
   token-painting view that is not listed WILL go red — that is the whitelist working, not a
   problem to route around. Conform it to `TokenThemed` and re-apply on
   `viewDidChangeEffectiveAppearance()` exactly as `TranscriptCardView` does; `.userMessage`'s fill
   is a real appearance-dependent leaf and must flip.

## Done when

- An assistant message in the tile has no border, no fill, and no title line.
- A user message has a fill and no border and no title line, distinguishable from the assistant's
  prose in BOTH appearances.
- Tool call, plan, diff and error still render as bordered titled cards with their status word.
- Prose text is still selectable (it is today; losing that is a regression).
- The full matrix is green, INCLUDING `--ui-contrast-check` and `--ui-baseline-check`, with no floor
  lowered and no check loosened.

## Verify

```
swift build 2>&1 | tail -20
./scripts/run-matrix.sh
```

**The border floor holds without being touched — prove it, don't assume it.**
`UIProbePixels.swift:454` sets `minimumCardBordersPerAppearance = 3` and the probe at line 381
counts views that satisfy `view is TranscriptCardView`. Six kinds are rendered; two stop being
`TranscriptCardView`; **four remain, which still clears the floor of 3.** So:

- **Do NOT lower `minimumCardBordersPerAppearance`.** There is no legitimate reason to, and doing it
  is exactly the matrix-weakening the runbook forbids.
- Print the actual post-change count and confirm it is ≥ 3 in the ledger note. If the fixture you
  are probing happens to contain only prose kinds, the honest fix is to add a structured kind to
  **the fixture** so the border probe has something to measure — never to lower the floor.

**Contrast is the real risk and it must be re-measured, not reasoned about.** Assistant prose moves
off `SurfaceToken.cardMessage` and onto whatever sits behind it — `contentBackdrop`, which
`ManagedAgentTileNSView.applyTokens()` paints `SurfaceToken.tileBody`. That is a NEW text/background
pair that the 452-pair sweep has never measured. `TextToken.textPrimary` on `SurfaceToken.tileBody`
must clear **4.5:1 in both appearances**. If it does not, the fix is the token value or the token
choice — **not** an exemption, and not putting the fill back. The contrast gate has zero exemptions
today and must still have zero after this.

**Required red-green witnesses** (observe each red at exit 1 against your FINAL code, and quote the
failure text verbatim beside the assertion, per house practice):

1. Prose given a border → the pixel/geometry assertion that says prose has no border goes red.
2. `.userMessage` routed to `TranscriptProseView`'s `.message` path (fill dropped) → the assertion
   that a user turn is distinguishable from an assistant turn goes red. **Without this witness the
   whole ticket is untested**, because "no chrome" is trivially satisfied by rendering nothing.
3. `.toolCall` routed to `TranscriptProseView` → the card-border floor assertion goes red (this is
   also the test that the floor is not vacuous).
4. `"TranscriptProseView"` removed from `tokenAdoptedOwners` → the sentinel sweep goes red on an
   unlisted owner.
5. `TokenThemed` conformance or the appearance override dropped from the prose view → the
   appearance-flip assertion goes red on `.userMessage`'s stale fill.
6. The `managedAgent.card.` identifier prefix changed on the prose view → the probes stop finding
   the transcript. This one is a **guard against silent un-probing**; if it does NOT go red, say so
   in the ledger, because it means the probes are matching on something weaker than you think.

**Baselines will move a lot, and that is expected.** Every committed PNG containing a transcript
changes. Inspect **several** diffs in BOTH appearances before blessing — not one — and re-run
`--ui-baseline-check` **at least 3 times** after blessing to prove determinism. The runbook records
why: baselines were once bulk-blessed off a 2-diff sample, one of which had captured a bad render.
Report the worst delta.

Vision/`--ui-tour-check` output is ADVISORY. It must never gate, and you must not certify the new
look by eye — render it, assert what is numerically assertable, and leave "does it read as a
conversation" to the owner.

## Watch out

- **Do not delete `SurfaceToken.cardMessage`** even if `.message` stops using it. Token deletion is
  not in this packet's scope, other checks enumerate the `cards` cases, and a token with no current
  call site is not a defect.
- **`Inset.card` on prose is horizontal padding, not a card.** Prose still needs to not touch the
  tile edge. Dropping the inset because "prose has no chrome" makes the text collide with the
  scroll view edge.
- **The vertical gap between entries is now doing more work than before.** The card stack's
  `Space.m` between entries was reading as "gap between boxes"; with prose unboxed it becomes the
  only thing separating two speakers' turns. If two consecutive prose turns are ambiguous at
  `Space.m`, that is a real finding — report it in the ledger with the measurement rather than
  inventing a separator rule, because a divider line between turns is a design decision the owner
  has not made.
- **`hasAmbiguousLayout`.** A view with no fill and no border and one wrapping label is the exact
  shape that ends up with an unconstrained height. The geometry gate covers this; do not add a bare
  `NSView()` spacer without a height constraint (see `ComponentLab.swift`'s recorded flake).
- Do not touch the compose row, the model picker, or anything else in the tile. The per-agent
  model/effort control is **P6.1**, a separate ticket, and the two must not be merged.
