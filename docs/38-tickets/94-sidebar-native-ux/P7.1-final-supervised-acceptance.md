## Goal

Accept the sidebar as a whole with the owner: containment, anatomy, truthfulness, identity, interaction, and lifecycle, on a real installed build.

Read `_DESIGN.md` and `_RUNBOOK.md` before editing, plus
`docs/38-tickets/91-agent-tile-ux/plan-sidebar-t3code-study.md` and

commit it independently.

## Approach

1. Quit the owner's instance, build and install a verified-fresh candidate, and confirm the bundle under review is the one built from the final tree.
2. Walk the whole surface at 220, 280, and 320 pt in both appearances with a realistic population: live agents, unconfirmed agents, parked work, a snoozed shelf, a fan-out parent, and a freshly spawned nameless agent.
3. Run the full matrix including the supervised surface legs, plus the focused checks five times each to catch order dependence, and bless any remaining baseline moves.
4. Record the owner's acceptance, every honest limit, and any follow-up ticket; then relaunch the app for the owner and confirm the running build is the accepted one.

## Watch out

- Execution is supervised: the loop must stop before it.
- Do not infer approval from a lack of comments.
- Baseline blessing requires the built-in Retina display (Color LCD) to be the Main Display; the
  retina-main check must pass first. Taste review may proceed beforehand, baseline approval may not.
- Quit the owner's running instance before any probe, build, or relaunch; it shares the store and
  tmux, and the boot probe hangs against a live instance.
- Do not open a new phase to fix a taste note found here; record it as a ticket.
- Never stage, commit, push, rebase, or alter queue/loop machinery outside the per-ticket workflow.
- If the packet conflicts with compiled reality or a locked decision, mark it `blocked` with concrete
  evidence; do not improvise a second architecture.
