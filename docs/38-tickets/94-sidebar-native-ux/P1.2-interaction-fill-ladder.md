# P1.2 — Interaction fill ladder

Phase: 1 · Containment and surface
Execution: **autonomous**
Depends on: P1.1

## Goal

Communicate hover, multi-selection, and route-active state with semantic fills, with selection quieter than hover.

## Approach

1. Resolve the row's fill from `AgentSurfaceRole` for four states: resting (unfilled), hover, multi-selected, and route-active.
2. Keep selection quieter than hover — hover is transient feedback, selection is a resting state — and assert the ordering by measurement.
3. Drive hover from a tracking area that survives scrolling and row reuse, and clear it when the pointer leaves the list or the window deactivates.
4. Assert that no row communicates state through a border, a shadow, or an inset stroke.

### Implementation sketch

```swift
let role: AgentSurfaceRole = isActive ? .rowActive
    : isSelected ? .rowSelected
    : isHovered ? .rowHover : .rowBase
layer?.backgroundColor = role.color.cgColor(for: theme)
```

The sketch establishes ownership and direction; adapt names to the compiled code, but do not replace
the architecture with a shortcut that couples derivation, presentation, and persistence.

## Done when

- All four states are distinguishable by measured fill in both appearances, with selection quieter than hover.
- Hover clears on exit, on scroll-induced reuse, and on window deactivation — no row is left lit.
- A row painting a border, shadow, or stroke for state fails a named assertion.
- Multi-selected and route-active are distinguishable from each other, not merely from resting.
- Relevant new behavior has a deterministic positive assertion and at least one required negative
  witness observed red against the final check.
- No existing matrix leg, assertion floor, contrast requirement, or I5 boundary is weakened.
