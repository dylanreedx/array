# P6.6 — Accessibility and motion sweep

Phase: 6 · Lifecycle and children
Execution: **autonomous**
Depends on: P6.5

## Goal

Make everything this program built reachable without a mouse or a screen, and quiet under Reduce Motion.

## Context and design direction

Read `_DESIGN.md` and `_RUNBOOK.md` before editing, plus
`docs/38-tickets/91-agent-tile-ux/plan-sidebar-t3code-study.md` and
`plan-sidebar-and-state-findings.md` for the evidence this program is built on. Surface is reserved
for interaction; the row's subject is its name; one owner answers what an agent is doing; a name is
never an identifier; no stock AppKit chrome belongs in the sidebar. Every geometry assertion runs at
220, 280, and 320 pt. This ticket is deliberately scoped so another fresh agent can verify and
commit it independently.

## Approach

1. Audit every new surface for role, label, value, and hierarchy: rows, status, unconfirmed and frozen durations, menu, filter band, bulk bar, shelves, and the divider.
2. Announce state changes once and only once — a status label is the live region, not the ticking duration beside it — and give the unconfirmed presentation an announcement that says why.
3. Honour Reduce Motion on every animation this program added, suppressing motion without removing the cue, and honour Increase Contrast where a fill carries meaning.
4. Assert the accessibility tree over the live view hierarchy for the whole corpus at all three widths, and assert that no element hidden by a tier is silently unreachable.

### Implementation sketch

```swift
// The label is the live region; the ticking number must not be announced each second.
statusLabel.setAccessibilityRole(.staticText)
elapsedLabel.setAccessibilityElement(false)
```

The sketch establishes ownership and direction; adapt names to the compiled code, but do not replace
the architecture with a shortcut that couples derivation, presentation, and persistence.

## Done when

- Every new surface exposes role, label, and value, asserted over the live tree at all three widths.
- A state change is announced once; a ticking duration is never announced repeatedly.
- Reduce Motion suppresses motion while keeping every cue, and Increase Contrast is honoured where a fill carries meaning.
- Anything a measured-fit tier hides is either still reachable or deliberately removed from the tree, with the reason in a comment.
- Relevant new behavior has a deterministic positive assertion and at least one required negative
  witness observed red against the final check.
- No existing matrix leg, assertion floor, contrast requirement, or I5 boundary is weakened.

## Watch out

- Wrapping a ticking duration in a live region makes a screen reader announce every second; that is worse than silence.
- An element hidden by a tier and left in the accessibility tree reads as present but unreachable.
- Do not add motion to satisfy a review note; the cue must survive Reduce Motion.
- Never stage, commit, push, rebase, or alter queue/loop machinery outside the per-ticket workflow.
- If the packet conflicts with compiled reality or a locked decision, mark it `blocked` with concrete
  evidence; do not improvise a second architecture.
