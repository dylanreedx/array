# P5.6 — Supervised native-interaction review

Phase: 5 · Native interaction
Execution: **supervised**
Depends on: P5.5, P4.5

## Goal

Review every interaction surface the sidebar now owns — menu, filter band, bulk bar, keyboard, resize, naming — as one native whole.

## Context and design direction

Read `_DESIGN.md` and `_RUNBOOK.md` before editing, plus
`docs/38-tickets/91-agent-tile-ux/plan-sidebar-t3code-study.md` and
`plan-sidebar-and-state-findings.md` for the evidence this program is built on. Surface is reserved
for interaction; the row's subject is its name; one owner answers what an agent is doing; a name is
never an identifier; no stock AppKit chrome belongs in the sidebar. Every geometry assertion runs at
220, 280, and 320 pt. This ticket is deliberately scoped so another fresh agent can verify and
commit it independently.

## Approach

1. Drive the context menu, scope control, search, bulk bar, rename, jump hints, keyboard traversal, and divider by hand in an installed candidate at all three widths.
2. Exercise VoiceOver end to end — row, status, menu, band, bar — plus Reduce Motion and full keyboard access, and confirm no stock AppKit chrome is reachable.
3. Confirm the naming ladder in practice: a fresh agent, a first prompt, a manual rename, and an explicit generated name, including a rename that races a generation.
4. Ask the owner whether the sidebar now feels like part of Continuum rather than a list bolted onto it, and record approval or corrections.

## Done when

- The owner explicitly approves, or sends corrections recorded as tickets.
- No stock `NSMenu`, `NSPopUpButton`, bezeled `NSTextField`, or default focus ring is reachable in the sidebar, asserted structurally and confirmed by inspection.
- VoiceOver, Reduce Motion, and full keyboard access are exercised on every new surface.
- Baselines moved by Phases 4 and 5 are blessed with `check-retina-main.swift` passing.
- Relevant new behavior has a deterministic positive assertion and at least one required negative
  witness observed red against the final check.
- No existing matrix leg, assertion floor, contrast requirement, or I5 boundary is weakened.

## Watch out

- Execution is supervised: the loop must stop before it.
- Do not infer approval from a lack of comments.
- Baseline blessing requires the built-in Retina display (Color LCD) to be the Main Display; the
  retina-main check must pass first. Taste review may proceed beforehand, baseline approval may not.
- Quit the owner's running instance before any probe, build, or relaunch; it shares the store and
  tmux, and the boot probe hangs against a live instance.
- Never stage, commit, push, rebase, or alter queue/loop machinery outside the per-ticket workflow.
- If the packet conflicts with compiled reality or a locked decision, mark it `blocked` with concrete
  evidence; do not improvise a second architecture.
