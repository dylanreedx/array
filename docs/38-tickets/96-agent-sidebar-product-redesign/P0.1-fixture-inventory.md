# P0.1 — production-frequency fixture inventory

The gate §6/P0.1 asks for: *"a declared fixture inventory maps each production flow to
its resulting row and surface."* This is that document, and
`--sidebar-production-corpus-check` fails if a declared flow is missing a row here or a
row here names a flow nothing runs.

Every "resulting row" below was **observed**, not predicted: the check drives the real
writers against real on-disk stores, lets the app's own
`refreshAgentSurfaces → buildAgentInboxRows → AgentContextIndex.build →
AgentInboxRowBuilder.rows` produce the row values, renders those values in a sized
`AgentInboxView`, and reads each fact off the rendered cell. Nothing in the corpus
constructs an `AgentInboxRow`.

## What this corpus establishes

1. **The row carries two facts and a glyph.** Of six bands, `meta` and `branch` are empty
   in **every one of 24 flows**, and the placement band never contains more than a
   project name. That is the owner's "too sparse", measured.
2. **`Project › Zone` never renders.** Not even for `exactPlacement`, whose tile really
   is on the canvas and geometrically inside the zone (`SidebarTree.tiles(for:)` assigns
   by tile centre). The Zone half of the design's line 1 does not exist today.
3. **The placement band is the FIRST thing sacrificed.** Three flows —
   `firstSendTitleFallback`, `generatedTitleLanded`, `longUnicodeRTL` — render no project
   at all, and all three are the rows with long titles: the measured-fit tier
   (`tier.drawsProject`) drops it at the default 280 pt width. §4.3's sacrifice order
   puts placement fourth and requires it to stay in tooltip/AX.
4. **Three of five terminal outcomes render no state**, and the other two collapse onto
   `Failed`. Success, interruption and cancellation are pixel-identical.
5. **No row anywhere carries a completion time.**
6. **Approval and input are the same word**, `Needs attention`.
7. **Provider identity is one of three Unicode glyphs** (`◈` / `✦` / `◇`) with no model
   text and no harness/provider separation; the unknown arm is the bare diamond.
8. **A blank ⌘K is durable work**, and an accepted image-only send keeps the sentinel.

## A refused verb that read as a rendered surface

An earlier version of the `settled` flow sent a prompt before settling. A send installs a
live runner, `canSettle` refuses while one exists, and the supervisor logged
`AgentSupervisor.settle: refusing blocked or already parked agent` on **every run** — while
this table still claimed the row reached the Settled surface. What it actually rendered
was an ordinary active full card.

The flow now settles without a send and **asserts the verb returned true**, which changed
the observed row: a settled row is SLIM, and a slim cell draws no placement band and no
provider mark. Every lifecycle verb's result is now checked rather than discarded.

## Coverage

All §6/P0.1 required shapes are now driven. 30 flows, 30 inventory rows, two-way parity
enforced by the check.

The one shape the design lists that is deliberately expressed differently: `unconfirmed`
is not its own flow because every non-observed spawn already renders `Unconfirmed` — a
dedicated case would duplicate, not add, coverage. `active` likewise: 24 of the 30 flows
are Active rows.
