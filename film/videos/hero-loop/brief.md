# Hero loop "In view": brief

Purpose: [social clip](../../purposes/social-clip.md) and a website hero (silent). Format:
[seamless loop](../../formats/seamless-loop.md) (this film defined it). Look: [caption band](../../looks/caption-band/README.md)
(no captions: a silent loop).

A silent, seamless 12 s loop for the README, release notes, social, email and an OG video. Three agents
work in one zone; one needs you; you answer, open a Terminal, everything lands green, and the next round
starts where this one began. The full bar table and the reducer steps are in script.md beside this file.
