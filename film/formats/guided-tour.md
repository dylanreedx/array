# Format: guided tour

Built: `videos/what-is-array` (24 s, r2, 2026-10-04) and `videos/canvas-tour`
(11 s, the pipeline's first sample). Both replay the website's demo
components, not the real app. That approach is retired (TASTE.md, 2026-10-04:
the mocks were off), so take the structure from them, never the picture.

A narrator (captions) walks one subject through a few ideas, one idea per
step. The camera is the narrator's finger: it pushes in on what the line is
about and pulls back to show where it sits.

## Beats (what-is-array, 120 BPM, a bar = 2 s)

| Beat | Bars | What happens | Job |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | 1 | One element alone, pushed in slowly, with a question about it | Curiosity in 2 s |
| Context | 2 | Pull back: the element sits in its real surroundings | Answer the question with the picture |
| Ideas | 3-10 | One idea per 1-2 bars: push in on a subject, everything else dimmed, a short caption, then a payoff state change (amber, then blue, then green) | Teach, with a payoff every 3-5 s |
| Lockup | 11-12 | Mark, wordmark, promise line, URL | Brand |

## What makes it work, and what didn't

- One subject per shot, at readable scale. The dim (Spotlight) shows where to look.
- Payoffs land on the beat, and the state colours resolve in a wave.
- Didn't work: captions in a bottom band, because the eyes jump between the
  band and the action (TASTE.md, 2026-10-04). Mocked UI also failed. Use real
  takes.
