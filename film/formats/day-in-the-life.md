# Format: day in the life

Built: `videos/full-day` ("A day in Array", 1:23 in r0 and 3:46 in v2 r1, 2026-10-05).

One user's working day, from morning to evening. Real takes are the chapters,
and a themed transition between them makes time pass. It builds the canvas up
as the day goes, then cleans it down at the end. This shows the whole product
without a feature list.

## Beats (full-day r0, 104 BPM)

| Beat | Time | What happens | Job |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Transition 1 | 0:00 | Sunrise: the clock runs to 8:45 and docks in a corner | Set the day |
| Morning | 0:04 | Yesterday's work is filed away. A new zone, a board, the first agents. The plan for the day comes from Jira. | Start from almost empty |
| Transition 2 | 0:27 | Coffee: the clock runs to 10:30 | Time passes |
| Late morning | 0:31 | Agents side by side, a browser, a live change | Build up |
| Transition 3 | 0:39 | Lunch: a card lists what finished while you were away | Work happened off camera |
| Afternoon | 0:44 | Tickets close themselves. A big ticket brings helpers. | Scale up |
| Transition 4 | 0:56 | Heads down: a time-lapse with motion trails | Lots of work, fast |
| Ship | 1:00 | Push and merge signals, then zoom out to everything | The fullest moment |
| Transition 5 | 1:09 | Sunset | Wind down |
| Evening | 1:14 | Close what's done. It's all in History. Empty zones. | A clean canvas |
| End | 1:23 | Lockup | Brand |

## What makes it work

- Build the thing while demoing it. Don't start zoomed out on a full canvas
  (TASTE.md, 2026-10-05).
- Each transition is unique, themed, poppy and under 5 s, and each one hides
  a cut between takes.
- Realistic load: many tickets, and several zones by evening. Every finished
  agent is followed up or closed on purpose (TASTE.md, v2 notes).
- Captions are pinned to the tile they're about, with clean icons and no emoji.
