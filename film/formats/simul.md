# Format: simul (UNBUILT)

Status: an idea, never built. Source: `.plans/68-launch-film/research/20-poppy-formats`
idea 2 (2026-10-15). Only an artifact pilot existed, and Dylan rejected all
the artifact pilots ("they all look horrible... cheesy", TASTE.md).

A metaphor carries the idea: a chess simultaneous exhibition. From overhead,
nine boards sit in a grid, and one player's hand moves across them, one move
per board on the beat. The camera dives into one board, and its edge
match-cuts to a tile edge, where the pieces become a diff. Pull out: the nine
boards are nine tiles. "You're not waiting on one game. You're playing nine."

## Beats (about 25 s, as proposed)

| Beat | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Hook | The overhead grid of boards, a move per board on the beat |
| Dive | Into one board; its edge match-cuts to a real tile's edge |
| Pull out | The boards are tiles; the grid is the canvas |
| Line | The promise, then the lockup |

## Open questions before building

- Flat top-down graphics or 3D? The proposal favoured flat (cheaper, and the
  grid is already a canvas, so the match cut is geometric).
- The match cut needs a real take whose tile grid lines up with the boards.
