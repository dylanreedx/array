# Dylan's taste, in his words

This is taste, not a house style. These are things Dylan said about specific films, with the date, so a
new video can learn from them without copying them. He has changed his mind before (pink was liked on
2026-10-05 and banned on 2026-10-15), so a recent note beats an old one, and a new note beats this page.
Quotes are verbatim, typos included. Dates are Dylan's local dates. Sources: the session transcripts and
`.plans/68-launch-film/`.
