# Purpose: general promo

Keeps Array in front of people after launch: a reason to look again, for
viewers who may have seen it before.

This file holds questions only. The format and the look are separate choices,
and they start fresh (README.md).

## Brief questions

1. Why now? What gives this video a reason to exist this week?
2. Who is it for: people who passed on Array before, current users, or both?
3. What one idea should stick after the viewer scrolls past?
4. Where will it run, and what length and aspect does that place reward?
5. What is the hook in the first second?
6. Which real workflow or moment carries the idea?
7. What must read with the sound off?
8. How does it end, and what is the call to action?
9. How is it different from the films we already have (videos/)? Name the difference.
10. Does it belong to a series, and what must stay the same across the series?
