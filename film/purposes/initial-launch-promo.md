# Purpose: initial launch promo

The first video most people will see of Array. It answers "why should I use
this?" for someone who has never heard of it, and makes them want to try it.

This file holds questions only. The format and the look are separate choices,
and they start fresh (README.md).

## Brief questions

1. What problem does the viewer feel today, in their own words? (which-tab used "Which tab was that?")
2. Who is it for, and who is it not for?
3. What is the one claim the film makes? It must be true and visible (claim visibility, not speed).
4. What does the first second show, so the film also works as its own thumbnail?
5. Where does it run first (X, LinkedIn, YouTube, the site hero), and how long can it be there?
6. Sound on or off by default? What must read with the sound off?
7. What is the turn: the moment the problem becomes the answer?
8. What real app moment is the payoff, and can the recorder film it?
9. What is the last thing on screen, and what should the viewer do next?
10. Which approved copy lines can it use, and what new lines does it need approved?
11. Which other launch films are references, and what exactly do we take from each (pacing, type, end card)?
12. What would make it feel generic (category clichés, sidebar lists, "mission control")? Avoid those.
