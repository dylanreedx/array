# Purpose: educational

Teaches one thing about Array to someone who will use it. Success is that the
viewer can do the thing afterwards.

This file holds questions only. The format and the look are separate choices,
and they start fresh (README.md).

## Brief questions

1. What is the one thing the viewer can do after watching? Say it as an action ("open an agent beside its browser").
2. Who is watching: never opened Array, just installed it, or uses it daily?
3. What do they already know: terminals, coding agents, which CLIs?
4. Where will they watch it: docs page, README, X, YouTube, inside the app? Sound on or off?
5. How long can it be before they stop watching? Name a number of seconds.
6. What is the first thing on screen, and why does it make them keep watching?
7. Which real features and labels appear? List them; each must be shipped, with its real name (docs/user/).
8. What is the order of steps, and what does each step teach?
9. What must be readable with the sound off?
10. What does the viewer do next: a doc link, a download, the next video?
11. What will we NOT show, because it would confuse or isn't shipped?
12. Which footage takes does it need, and can the recorder produce them today?
