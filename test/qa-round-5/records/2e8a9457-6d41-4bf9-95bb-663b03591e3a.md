## What is broken

## Why
- Ghostty exposes separate APIs for committed text and preedit text, and the local wrapper only used the committed C text path before this fix. /Users/dylan/Library/Mobile Documents/com~apple~CloudDocs/personal/ghostty-src/include/ghostty.h:1103
- Upstream Ghostty routes AppKit key events through NSTextInputClient methods before final key encoding, which is the missing layer for Korean, Japanese, Pinyin, and dead-key input. /Users/dylan/Library/Mobile Documents/com~apple~CloudDocs/personal/ghostty-src/macos/Sources/Ghostty/Surface View/SurfaceView_AppKit.swift:1818

## Fix
- `GhosttyTerminalView` now accumulates `insertText` callbacks during `keyDown(with:)`, synchronizes marked text through `ghostty_surface_preedit`, and forwards normal or composed key events through `ghostty_surface_key`. Sources/ContinuumRevived/TerminalEngine/GhosttyTerminalView.swift:54
- `GhosttyTerminalView` now conforms to `NSTextInputClient` with marked range, selected range, attributed substring, IME rect, `insertText`, `setMarkedText`, `unmarkText`, and `doCommand(by:)` implementations. Sources/ContinuumRevived/TerminalEngine/GhosttyTerminalView.swift:449
- The smoke timeline now inserts `printf 'ime-é-ok\n'`, exercises marked text followed by committed text, and gates success on `imeInsertedTextSeen` plus `markedTextCleared`. Sources/ContinuumRevived/App/ContinuumApp.swift:1054
