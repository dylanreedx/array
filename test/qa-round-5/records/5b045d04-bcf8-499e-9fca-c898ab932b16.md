## What is broken
- The QA smoke harness had named visual flows but no in-process timing or resident-memory baseline path, so `canvas-drag-resize`, launch, terminal stress, and Cmd-K churn could not produce perf regression artifacts. Sources/ContinuumRevived/App/ContinuumApp.swift:853

## Why
- The existing QA capture helper only wrote PNG and manifest evidence when `CONTINUUM_QA_CAPTURE` was set, and it did not evaluate metric values against thresholds. Sources/ContinuumRevived/App/QACapture.swift:54
